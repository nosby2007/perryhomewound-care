Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how health information may be used and disclosed and how you can access it.

Compliance review required: This implementation draft must be reviewed and approved by Perry Home Wound Care’s privacy/compliance lead and legal counsel before production publication.

Proposed effective date: July 28, 2026

Please review this notice carefully. Perry Home Wound Care is committed to protecting the privacy and security of health information.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Provide an electronic or paper copy of your health record.
  • Correct health information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Provide a list of certain disclosures of your health information.
  • Limit certain uses or disclosures, although we may not be required to agree.
  • Communicate with you in a particular way or at a particular location.
  • Provide a paper copy of this notice.
  • Allow a person with legal authority to exercise these rights for you.

Our uses and disclosures

We may use or share health information for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations, including coordinating with clinicians, wound centers, hospitals, facilities, pharmacies, laboratories, health plans, and caregivers involved in your care when permitted by law.

We may also use or disclose information for public health and safety activities, health oversight, workers’ compensation, law-enforcement requests, judicial proceedings, organ donation, research when authorized, and other purposes permitted or required by law.

For uses not otherwise permitted by law, we will request written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing, except to the extent we have already acted on it.

Our responsibilities

  • Maintain the privacy and security of protected health information.
  • Notify affected individuals when required after a breach of unsecured protected health information.
  • Follow the duties and privacy practices described in the notice currently in effect.
  • Provide a revised notice if our privacy practices materially change.

Questions or complaints

You may complain to us if you believe your privacy rights were violated. You may also submit a complaint to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
