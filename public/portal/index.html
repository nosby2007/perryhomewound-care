Your portal account is personal. Keep your password and verification codes private, sign out on shared devices, and contact Perry Home Wound Care if you believe another person accessed your account. Patient Portal Privacy & Security

This portal contains confidential health and billing information. Do not share your password, authenticator codes, or active session with another person.

Use a trusted device, sign out when finished, and protect any reports or documents you download to your device. Contact PHWC promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

The portal is informational and does not replace emergency care or direct clinical communication. Do not change wound treatment solely because a field appears different unless your PHWC clinical team instructed you to do so.
