UNDERSTANDING HOME WOUND CARE

What patients and families should know.

Wound care at home is more than a dressing change. It includes consistent assessment, monitoring for important changes, education, pressure relief strategies, skin protection, coordination with the treating provider, and practical support for the patient and caregiver.

Perry Home Wound Care brings this wound-focused support into the patient’s home, helping families understand the plan and giving referring clinicians a clearer view of how the wound is progressing between appointments.

Our mission is to make wound care easier to understand and easier to continue after the patient leaves the hospital, wound center, rehabilitation setting, or physician office.

We aim to be a dependable extension of the patient’s healthcare team—bringing wound-focused assessment, education, documentation, and communication into the home while respecting the existing plan of care.

OUR APPROACH

A simple process for patients, families, and referrers.

1

Referral or Intake

We receive the request and basic information needed to understand the wound care need.

2

Clinical Review

Relevant orders, wound history, recent documentation, and service requirements are reviewed.

3

Home Visit

The clinician assesses the wound, the home environment, caregiver needs, and practical barriers to the plan.

4

Care & Education

Wound care is provided within the authorized plan, with clear education for the patient and caregiver.

5

Follow-Up & Coordination

Progress and important changes are documented and communicated to the appropriate care team.
