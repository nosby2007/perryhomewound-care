HOW PHWC CAN SUPPORT THE AGENCY

Extra wound focus when the case needs it.

Complex wounds can demand more wound-specific assessment, education, and coordination. PHWC can be used as an additional wound-care resource within the appropriate clinical and payer pathway.

01

Complex wound follow-up

Wound and periwound assessment, measurements, drainage review, dressing-plan support, and documentation.

02

Staff & caregiver support

Education around skin protection, pressure reduction, dressing technique, and escalation triggers.

03

Provider coordination

Support communication with the ordering or treating provider when meaningful clinical changes require review.
