FACILITY SUPPORT

A wound-care resource for residents and staff.

Residential care staff often recognize skin changes early. PHWC can help turn those observations into a structured wound-care pathway with assessment, education, and coordinated follow-up.

01

Resident wound assessment

Evaluate wound characteristics, surrounding skin, drainage, pressure points, and care-plan factors that may affect healing.

02

Pressure & skin prevention

Education around repositioning, heel offloading, moisture control, support surfaces, and early skin changes.

03

Staff & family education

Practical guidance that helps caregivers understand the wound plan, dressing schedule, and when to escalate changes.

FACILITY WORKFLOW

Easy to activate when a resident needs wound care.

Identify a resident

Staff, family, or the treating provider identifies a wound or skin concern requiring wound-focused follow-up.

Send the referral

Provide available clinical information, orders, payer details, and the facility point of contact.

Coordinate the visit

PHWC reviews service appropriateness and coordinates scheduling with the resident, representative, and facility.

Keep the team informed

Relevant findings and follow-up needs are communicated through the established care pathway.

Need wound support for a resident?

Start a referral or contact PHWC about a facility partnership for wound-focused resident care.
