HOW WE SUPPORT YOUR PRACTICE

A practical extension of the care plan.

Primary care teams often identify wounds first but may not have the capacity for frequent home-based wound follow-up. PHWC can help close that gap while keeping the referring team informed.

01

Home wound assessment

Focused wound and periwound assessment, measurements, drainage review, skin integrity evaluation, and documentation.

02

Education & adherence support

Reinforce dressing instructions, offloading or pressure-reduction strategies, and caregiver understanding when clinically appropriate.

03

Escalation & coordination

Communicate important changes back to the appropriate provider and support timely next-step planning.

REFERRAL WORKFLOW

Simple for the practice. Clear for the patient.

Identify the need

Your team identifies a patient who would benefit from wound-focused home support.

Send the referral

Provide the available order, wound information, payer information, and relevant care-plan documentation.

PHWC coordinates intake

We review the referral, contact the patient or caregiver, and confirm the appropriate next step.

Clinical communication

Relevant wound findings and care coordination are communicated according to the patient’s plan and referral pathway.
