DISCHARGE CONTINUITY

Close the gap between discharge instructions and real-life wound care.

Patients and caregivers may leave the hospital with multiple instructions, supplies, appointments, and new responsibilities. PHWC can provide wound-focused support in the home while coordinating with the existing medical team.

01

Post-discharge wound review

Wound and periwound assessment, drainage review, measurement, and evaluation of how the discharge plan is working at home.

02

Patient & caregiver education

Reinforce wound-care steps, skin protection, pressure reduction, supply use, and clinically appropriate warning signs.

03

Care coordination

Support communication with the relevant provider, home health team, facility, or follow-up service when the wound plan needs review.

DISCHARGE WORKFLOW

A clearer handoff from inpatient care to home.

Identify before discharge

Case management identifies a patient with a wound that may need structured home follow-up.

Send clinical information

Referral includes available wound details, orders, discharge instructions, payer information, and provider contacts.

Coordinate after discharge

PHWC reviews the referral and contacts the patient or caregiver to establish the appropriate next step.

Maintain continuity

Relevant wound findings and concerns are communicated through the established post-discharge care pathway.

Planning a discharge for a patient with a complex wound?

Send the referral before discharge so the home follow-up pathway can be reviewed as early as possible.
