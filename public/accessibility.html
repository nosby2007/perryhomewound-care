ACCESS FOR EVERY VISITOR

Accessibility

We want patients, caregivers, and partners to be able to use our website and contact our team.

Our commitment

Perry Home Wound Care is working to improve keyboard access, readable contrast, responsive layouts, form labels, alternative text, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

Need assistance?

If a page or form is difficult to use, contact us and tell us the page, the problem encountered, and your preferred way to receive the information. We will make reasonable efforts to provide an accessible alternative.
